# Site updates (illustrative example)

> This profile is a worked example built from a described persona, not from a
> real writer's samples. Replace it with a real one (see README), or delete it.

## When to use this profile
A construction project manager writing to their own team and subs in email or
chat. Reader knows the job site, the drawings, and the open items.

## Style summary
Short sentences, often two clauses joined by "so". Names the vendor or the
drawing number instead of saying "the vendor" or "the plan". States the hold-up,
who owns it, and the date in the first two sentences. Complains in passing, not
as the subject of the sentence ("still waiting on their number", "third time
they've rescheduled"). Ends with what they need from the reader, or with
nothing. Contractions throughout. No bullet lists under five items; a real list
is fine. Never writes "please be advised" or "at your earliest convenience".

## Exemplars
> We're still waiting on the subcontractor's revised number. They're targeting
> Friday, so bid leveling may be held up until then.

> Steel showed up a day late, so decking pours Thursday now. Flag me if that
> hits your inspection window.

> Need the RFI answers by Wednesday or the schedule slides again.
