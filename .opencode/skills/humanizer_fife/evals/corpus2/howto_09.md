# How to Read More Books: A Practical System

Want to read more books but can't find the time? This guide unveils a meticulous system that has helped thousands of readers transform their habits and unlock their reading potential.

## Track Everything

First and foremost, what gets measured gets managed. Keep a simple reading log — a notebook or an app — and record every page. Moreover, tracking showcases your progress and reinforces the habit loop.

## Lower the Bar

Furthermore, commit to reading just one page per day. This may seem trivial, but it's crucial: momentum matters more than volume. Once you begin, you'll often continue for chapters.

## Curate Ruthlessly

Additionally, give yourself permission to abandon books. A meticulous reader is not someone who finishes every book, but someone who never stays stuck in a mediocre one. Life is too short for books that don't captivate you.

## Engineer Your Environment

Moreover, keep a book on your nightstand, in your bag, and by the couch. Replace twenty minutes of scrolling with twenty minutes of pages. Furthermore, always know what your next book will be.

In conclusion, reading more isn't about discipline — it's about systems. Happy reading!
