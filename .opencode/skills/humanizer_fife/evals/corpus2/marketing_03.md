# LearnLoop: Where Curious Minds Become Skilled Minds

The internet promised us unlimited learning. What we got was unlimited video playlists. LearnLoop is different — it's the online learning platform engineered around one question: what actually makes skills stick?

At its core, LearnLoop combines three proven ingredients: bite-sized lessons, spaced repetition, and real projects. Bite-sized lessons respect your attention. Spaced repetition defeats forgetting. Real projects turn knowledge into portfolio pieces.

But what truly separates LearnLoop is the loop itself. When you finish a lesson, the system schedules your review at the perfect moment. When you struggle, it adjusts. When you master a skill, it connects you with learners one step behind — because teaching cements learning.

The outcomes are remarkable: 87% of learners complete their courses, compared to an industry average under 13%. Moreover, one in three graduates reports a promotion or new role within a year.
