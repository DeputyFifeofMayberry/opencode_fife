# Review: The RoboVac X8 Is Close to Effortless

I've lived with the RoboVac X8 for a month now, and it has genuinely changed how my household thinks about cleaning. This isn't to say it's perfect — it isn't — but it's the first robot vacuum I haven't wanted to return to its box.

Right out of the box, setup takes ten minutes: attach the brushes, connect to Wi-Fi, and let the lidar map your floors. The mapping is remarkably accurate, and the app's room labels are easy to edit. At its core, the X8's 4,000 Pa suction handles pet hair on both hardwood and medium carpet without complaint.

But what truly impresses is the self-emptying base. Moreover, the 2.5-liter bag holds about seven weeks of debris, meaning the robot largely maintains itself.

It's worth noting the limitations. The mopping function is a damp cloth, not a scrubber. Furthermore, dark carpets occasionally confuse the cliff sensors, and the robot once wandered into a cable nest and stopped, requiring rescue.

Would I recommend it? For homes with pets and mostly hard floors, absolutely — at $549, it's an investment that quietly pays you back in reclaimed hours.
