# How to Brew the Perfect Cup of Tea

Brewing exceptional tea is an art that anyone can master. In this comprehensive guide, we delve into the nuances that elevate a simple cup into a truly remarkable experience.

## Water: The Foundation

First and foremost, start with fresh, cold water — preferably filtered. Moreover, water temperature matters immensely: delicate green teas require 70–80°C, while robust black teas demand a full rolling boil.

## Timing: The Crucial Variable

Steeping time is paramount. Over-steeping releases bitter tannins that mask the tea's intricate character. As a general rule, green teas need one to three minutes, oolongs three to five, and black teas four to five. Additionally, always cover your cup while steeping to retain the volatile aromatics.

## The Leaves Themselves

Furthermore, invest in loose-leaf tea whenever possible. Tea bags often contain dust and fannings that showcase none of the leaf's nuanced flavor. One teaspoon per cup is the golden ratio.

## Savor the Moment

In conclusion, tea brewing rewards attention and patience. By meticulously controlling water, temperature, and time, you unlock a world of flavor hidden in a single leaf. Happy sipping!
