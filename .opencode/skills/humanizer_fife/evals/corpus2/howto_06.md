# How to Handle Basic Car Maintenance Yourself

You don't need to be a mechanic to keep your car healthy. A handful of simple checks, done monthly, can prevent the most expensive failures. Here's your routine.

## Check the Essentials Monthly

First and foremost, walk around the car and inspect tire pressure and tread. Additionally, check oil level, coolant, and windshield washer fluid — your owner's manual shows exactly where each reservoir lives.

## Learn the Warning Lights

Next, it's important to understand your dashboard. A yellow light asks for attention soon; a red light means stop driving. Moreover, never ignore the oil pressure or temperature lights — driving on either can destroy an engine.

## Follow the Service Schedule

Furthermore, stick to the manufacturer's maintenance schedule for oil changes, air filters, and brake inspections. Keeping a simple log in your glovebox makes resale easier, too.

## Know When to Stop

Some jobs — brake work, anything involving the fuel system — belong to professionals. Knowing the difference is part of maintenance.

With these habits in place, your car will reward you with years of reliable service. Happy driving!
