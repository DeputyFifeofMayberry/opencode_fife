# SafeHaul Driver App 4.0 Release Notes

## Overview

Version 4.0 is a transformative update for SafeHaul drivers, meticulously redesigned around the daily workflow of the road. Every screen, every interaction, and every notification has been rethought to unlock safer, more efficient hauls.

## Key Features

### Smart Route Log

Moreover, the new route log captures hours-of-service automatically, showcasing remaining drive time before you ask. Furthermore, it flags upcoming rest stops that match your schedule.

### Digital Inspection

Additionally, the pre-trip inspection is now guided with photo checkpoints — a testament to our safety-first philosophy. Defects route directly to maintenance with location tags.

### Offline Maps

Furthermore, offline navigation now covers the entire national network, ensuring you're never lost in a dead zone.

## Improvements

- 50% faster app startup on older devices
- One-tap detention timer at pickup and drop-off
- Fuel price comparison along the active route

## Known Issues

Some Android 12 devices may experience slow camera launch during inspections; a fix is planned for 4.0.1.

We're excited for you to experience 4.0. Safe travels, and thank you for the crucial work you do.
