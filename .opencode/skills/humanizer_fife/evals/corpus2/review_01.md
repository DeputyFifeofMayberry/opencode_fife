# Review: The Keychron K2 Mechanical Keyboard

After three weeks with the Keychron K2, I can confidently say it's one of the most compelling compact mechanical keyboards on the market — though it isn't without compromises.

Right out of the box, the K2 makes a statement. The aluminum frame feels substantial, and the RGB backlighting is vibrant without being gaudy. At its core, the K2 uses Gateron G Pro red switches, which deliver a smooth, quiet typing experience ideal for both long writing sessions and late-night gaming.

But what truly sets the K2 apart is its versatility. The keyboard connects via Bluetooth to three devices simultaneously, toggling with a single keypress. Moreover, it works across Windows, macOS, and Linux with a physical switch — no remapping software required.

It's worth noting the shortcomings. The stock keycaps are thin ABS that develop shine quickly. Additionally, the 75% layout removes the dedicated number pad, which spreadsheets users may miss. Battery life, while solid at about a week, trails some competitors.

Ultimately, the Keychron K2 earns its place as a top pick for anyone seeking a compact mechanical keyboard. It combines premium feel with genuine flexibility, and at around $90, it delivers value that's hard to beat.
