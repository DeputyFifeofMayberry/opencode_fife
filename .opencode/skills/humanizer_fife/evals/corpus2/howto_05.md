# How to Train for Your First 5K

Running your first 5K is an achievable goal for almost anyone, and a simple eight-week plan can carry you from the couch to the finish line. Here's how to build it.

## Week 1–2: Run-Walk Intervals

First and foremost, alternate one minute of easy jogging with two minutes of walking for 20 minutes, three times per week. Additionally, resist the urge to run faster — this phase builds your aerobic base.

## Weeks 3–5: Extend the Running

Next, flip the ratio: two minutes running, one minute walking. Moreover, add a fourth weekly session if you're feeling strong, keeping every run at a conversational pace.

## Weeks 6–8: Approach the Distance

Furthermore, extend your longest weekly session until you're covering three miles, mixing in short runs on the other days. It's fine to walk during the race itself — finishing is the goal.

## Race Day

Start slower than feels necessary, soak in the atmosphere, and save a little energy for the final stretch.

With eight consistent weeks behind you, that finish line is yours. Happy running!
