Subject: Recapping DevCon: What We Learned and What's Next

Hi team,

I'm back from DevCon, and I wanted to share the highlights before the details fade. It was a genuinely valuable three days.

Three themes dominated the conference. First, edge computing has moved from buzzword to production reality — two case studies showed meaningful latency wins. Second, developer experience is now a buying criterion, not a nice-to-have; vendors are competing on it. Third, everyone is shipping AI features, though remarkably few can explain their unit economics.

Moreover, I attended a standout workshop on migration strategies for legacy queues, which maps directly onto our phase-two plans. My notes and the slide decks are in the shared folder.

Additionally, I made contact with the platform team at Northwind — they solved the exact multi-region problem we've been circling, and they've offered a call.

It's worth noting that my biggest takeaway had nothing to do with technology: the teams shipping fastest all had boring, disciplined release processes.

I'll present a full debrief at Thursday's sync. In the meantime, the photos are in the usual place.

Best,
