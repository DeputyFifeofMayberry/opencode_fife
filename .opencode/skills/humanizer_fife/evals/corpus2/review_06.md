# Review: The FlexiSpot E7 Standing Desk Grows on You — Literally

A standing desk is a simple product, which is why the differences between them matter so much. After six weeks with the FlexiSpot E7, I've learned what those differences are, and why this frame deserves its strong reputation.

Right out of the box, the E7 makes a structural statement: a dual-motor, three-stage frame rated for 220 pounds, with crossbars that eliminate the wobble that plagues cheaper desks. At its core, that stability is the whole story — typing at standing height produces zero bounce, even with dual monitors.

But what truly enhances daily use are the four memory presets. Moreover, the motors are quiet enough for calls, transitioning between sitting and standing in about 15 seconds.

It's worth noting the details. The desktop is sold separately, which adds cost and a decision. Additionally, the control panel's cable routing is fiddly, and the anti-collision sensor, while present, is conservative.

The verdict: at roughly $500 for the frame, the E7 is a foundation worth building on. Would I recommend it? For anyone investing in a desk they'll touch fifty times a day, absolutely — stability is not a luxury.
