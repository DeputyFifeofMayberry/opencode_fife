# How to Set Up a Password Manager in One Evening

If you're still reusing passwords, a password manager is the single highest-impact security upgrade you can make. Here's how to get set up in under an hour.

## Pick a Manager

First and foremost, choose a reputable password manager — 1Password, Bitwarden, and Dashlane are solid starting points. Additionally, consider whether you need family sharing, which most paid tiers include.

## Create Your Master Password

Next, it's critical to craft a master password that is long, unique, and memorable. A four-word passphrase works well. Moreover, this is the one password you must never reuse anywhere.

## Import and Replace

Furthermore, most managers can import passwords exported from your browser automatically. Start by updating your most important accounts — email and banking first — and let the manager generate strong replacements.

## Turn On Two-Factor Everywhere

Finally, enable two-factor authentication on your email and financial accounts, and store backup codes in your manager's secure notes.

With these steps complete, you'll have a single vault of strong, unique passwords. Happy securing!
