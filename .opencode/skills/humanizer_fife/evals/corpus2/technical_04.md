# FieldNotes Mobile 5.2 Release Notes

## Overview

Version 5.2 is our most significant mobile release of the year, packed with transformative features designed to empower field researchers to unlock their full productivity, even far from connectivity.

## New Features

### Offline Map Packs

FieldNotes 5.2 introduces downloadable map packs covering entire survey regions. Moreover, annotations made offline sync automatically the moment connectivity returns — ensuring no observation is ever lost.

### Voice-to-Note Transcription

Furthermore, the new voice capture converts spoken observations into structured notes using on-device transcription, so your data never leaves your phone.

### Enhanced Media Handling

Photos now embed GPS, compass heading, and weather at capture time. Additionally, bulk import from SD cards preserves the metadata chain.

## Improvements

- 40% faster sync on large surveys
- Redesigned species picker with recent-use memory
- Dark mode across all screens

## Known Issues

Battery consumption may increase during extended offline sessions; we recommend power banks for multi-day surveys.

The future of field research is mobile, and we're honored to build it with you. Happy surveying!
