# Review: The Aurora Coffee Grinder

I spent several weeks with the Aurora burr grinder.

Right out of the box, the Aurora makes a statement. The brushed stainless steel body is attractive and well engineered. The 40mm conical burrs deliver remarkably consistent particle size, which matters for extraction.

The Aurora is also versatile: the 48-step adjustment covers espresso, pour-over, and French press with precise control over grind size. The anti-static grounds bin significantly reduces mess and makes daily use easier.

At $299 it occupies a competitive price point; budget-conscious buyers may find comparable performance for less. The hopper holds only 200 grams, so frequent refills may frustrate heavy users.

The Aurora is a solid option for enthusiasts who want to upgrade their coffee setup. Solid engineering and thoughtful design earn it a place, even in a market this crowded. Would I recommend it? For the right user, absolutely. It suits someone who cares about grind consistency more than the $299 price.
