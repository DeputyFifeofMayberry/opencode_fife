# Review: The Aurora Coffee Grinder

I spent several weeks with the Aurora burr grinder. It's a well-built machine with two real drawbacks.

The brushed stainless body looks good, and the 40mm conical burrs produce a very consistent particle size, which is what you want for good extraction. The 48-step adjustment covers espresso, pour-over, and French press, and the anti-static grounds bin keeps the mess down.

At $299 it isn't cheap, and buyers on a budget can find comparable performance for less. The hopper holds only 200 grams, so heavy users will be refilling it often.

Would I recommend it? For the right user, absolutely. It suits someone who cares about grind consistency more than the price.
