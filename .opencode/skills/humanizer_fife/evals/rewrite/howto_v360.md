# How to set up a home network

Setting up a home network is straightforward: pick the equipment, configure it, and keep it maintained.

## Choose the right equipment

Get a router that supports Wi-Fi 6 with strong security features and enough coverage for your space. For larger homes, consider a mesh system instead, which keeps connectivity consistent in every room.

## Configure your network

Change the default administrator password first. Then enable WPA3 encryption, and set up a guest network to keep your primary devices isolated.

A centrally placed router gives you stronger signal in every room and fewer dead zones.

## Optimize and maintain

Keep it maintained after that: update the firmware when the manufacturer releases it, keep an eye on the connected-device list, and restart the equipment now and then.
