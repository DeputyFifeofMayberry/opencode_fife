# Review: The Aurora Coffee Grinder

I spent several weeks with the Aurora burr grinder, and I'm happy to share my thoughts on it.

Right out of the box, the Aurora makes a statement. The brushed stainless steel body is attractive and well engineered. The grinder uses 40mm conical burrs that deliver remarkably consistent particle size, which is crucial for achieving optimal extraction.

The Aurora is also versatile. Whether you're brewing espresso, pour-over, or French press, the 48-step adjustment mechanism offers precise control over grind size. The anti-static grounds bin significantly reduces mess, a small detail that helps in daily use.

The grinder has its shortcomings. At $299, it occupies a competitive price point, and budget-conscious buyers may find comparable performance for less. The hopper, while attractive, holds only 200 grams, so frequent refills may frustrate heavy users.

The Aurora is a good option for enthusiasts who care about their coffee. It combines solid engineering with thoughtful design, and while it faces stiff competition, it earns its place in a crowded market. Would I recommend it? For the right user, absolutely.
