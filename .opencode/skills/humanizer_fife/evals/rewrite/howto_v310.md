A home network comes together in three steps: pick the equipment, configure it, and keep it maintained.

## Choose the Right Equipment

Get a router that supports Wi-Fi 6 and covers your space. For larger homes, a mesh system prevents dead zones at the far end.

## Configure Your Network

Change the default administrator password first. Enable WPA3 encryption so the network is secure, then set up a guest network to keep your own devices isolated. A router placed centrally strengthens the signal in every room.

## Optimize and Maintain

Update the firmware when the manufacturer releases it, check which devices are connected, and restart the equipment now and then.

With those steps done, the network is up and secure.
