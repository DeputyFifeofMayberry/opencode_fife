# 3.6.0 rewrite — ai_essay_remote_work.md

Distributed teams are now a permanent feature of work, not a stopgap.

Communication keeps everyone aligned. Trust reduces the need for oversight. Technology provides the infrastructure that connects people. Culture matters more than tools: when team members feel empowered to share ideas, innovation follows, and when leaders are transparent, engagement increases.

The transition has real costs. Timezone differences and the loss of spontaneous conversation affect distributed teams, and organizations that invest in deliberate communication practices consistently outperform those that do not.
