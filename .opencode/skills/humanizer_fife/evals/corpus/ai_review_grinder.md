# Review: The Aurora Coffee Grinder

I recently had the opportunity to spend several weeks with the Aurora burr grinder, and I'm excited to share my thoughts on this intriguing piece of equipment.

Right out of the box, the Aurora makes a statement. The brushed stainless steel body isn't just aesthetically pleasing — it's a testament to thoughtful engineering. At its core, this grinder features 40mm conical burrs that deliver remarkably consistent particle size, which is crucial for achieving optimal extraction.

But what truly sets the Aurora apart is its versatility. Whether you're brewing espresso, pour-over, or French press, the 48-step adjustment mechanism offers precise control over grind size. Additionally, the anti-static grounds bin significantly reduces mess, a small detail that makes a big difference in daily use.

It's worth noting that the grinder isn't without its shortcomings. At $299, it occupies a competitive price point, and budget-conscious buyers may find comparable performance for less. The hopper, while attractive, holds only 200 grams — frequent refills may frustrate heavy users.

Ultimately, the Aurora is a compelling option for enthusiasts seeking to elevate their coffee experience. It combines solid engineering with thoughtful design, and while it faces stiff competition, it earns its place in the crowded grinder market. Would I recommend it? For the right user, absolutely.
