## 1. How to read this ledger

Every material source observation receives an evidence ID and a claim/finding link. Paths are relative to the named repository unless absolute. Repository observations were made at the pinned revision shown. External measured results belong to their original implementation and evaluation, never to `opencode_fife`.

Evidence classes:

- `REPO-VERIFIED`: directly inspected in the pinned target fork.
- `UPSTREAM-VERIFIED`: directly inspected in pinned upstream or competitor open-source code.
- `MEASURED`: quantitative empirical result reported by a source with an evaluation design.
- `DOCUMENTED`: official documentation/design statement, not runtime proof.
- `THIRD-PARTY`: secondary or non-official source.
- `INFERRED`: interpretation derived from evidence rather than directly observed.
- `UNKNOWN`: required evidence was unavailable.

Outcome strength is recorded separately in the reports as `Verified mechanism only`, `Observational association`, `Controlled effect`, or `Undetermined`. In particular, a verified mechanism is not evidence that accepted-task quality improved.

**Finding totals:** 7 substantiated gaps, 4 opportunities, 6 unknowns. The gap analysis contains the complete records, alternatives, and priorities.

## 9. Stopping rationale

Research stopped at **evidence saturation for the requested decision**, not at a claim of exhaustive public coverage. Additional broad landscape searches were yielding repeated mechanisms rather than new evidence that would change the fork findings. The two concrete effort findings, the bounded transcript contract, the scoped containment absence, the measurement/assurance gaps, and the upstream-maintenance exposure were each reconciled with alternatives. Remaining questions require unavailable raw data, deployment access, runtime execution, or a separate exhaustive equivalence audit; more web searching would not resolve them.

No major rewrite is evidence-justified. Such a proposal would need controlled accepted-task benefits, explicit upstream/configuration/session/plugin/provider compatibility analysis, migration and rollback evidence, and a funded maintenance burden. Those thresholds were not met.
